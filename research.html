How to read the tiers. A Church entity is the owner of record. B The LLC's Sunbiz officers, agent or shared address tie it to a person or company that news reporting names as a Scientology parishioner buyer. C Probable: an address or manager link only. Check these before relying on them. No link found. That is not proof of independence; it means nothing in these sources connects the owner.

Gaps and caveats

• Coverage area: lat 27.955–27.982, lon −82.810 to −82.784. That is downtown plus North Marina; it leaves out Clearwater Beach and outlying church properties. Homestead (owner-occupied home) parcels were excluded.

• The counts don't match the Times. This finds 288 parcels; the Times reports about 200 downtown properties tied to the church or its members, plus the church's own holdings. The difference: condo units (26 at 331 Cleveland) and small lots (48 on Grove St) count separately here, and land-trust and C-tier rows add more. Some Times-identified LLCs are probably missed, because their managers don't match any name in the reporting I could access.

• LLCs disclose managers, not owners. Florida does not record beneficial owners. A registered agent such as attorney Steven Hayes can serve many unrelated clients, so an agent-only link is weaker evidence than a named manager.

• Taxable value is the county figure; it is $0 on exempt religious parcels and is not a sale price.

• Not covered: who individual business owners are. This research looks only at who owns the property. For current ownership, use the live map at Clear Path.
